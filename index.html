FROM PIXELS TO YOUR WALL

A clock in a few clicks.

Pick a shape or upload a filled SVG. Adjust the diameter, finish, thickness, and shaft opening to match your quartz movement.

Export the STL, open it in your slicer with the flat side down, and print without supports. Texture and markers add up to 0.8 mm above the face. Preview hands are illustrative and aren’t included in the print.

The STL follows smooth contours sampled at about 0.15 mm, with a precisely rounded shaft opening. Check disconnected SVG pieces and your mechanism’s shaft length before printing. Colors are a filament preview.
