    FROM YOUR SCREEN TO YOUR WALL

    Your clock in 3 steps.

    1. Pick a shape or upload your SVG.

      Your SVG keeps its colors and details. Rotate or flip it to get the right view.

    2. Make it yours.

      Choose your size and hour numbers. Detail relief raises the drawing so it shows up in a one-color print. Preview hands are for display only.

    3. Choose how you’ll print.

      Use One-color print for STL, or Bambu A1 · colors for a colored 3MF. Check the size, then download. In Bambu Studio, open the 3MF as a project and assign your filaments before slicing.

    Want to finish later? Save project keeps your settings and original SVG in one editable .clock file.

    Clock movement & printing tips

    Use Print mesh to inspect the actual surface, center hole, and thickness. Check model also finds separate pieces and checks your build plate. SVG details add up to 2 mm of relief; hour details add up to 0.8 mm above that. Choose a movement shaft long enough for the printed face and fixing nut.

    With Raised box and Flat front on bed, the front prints on the plate and the open box grows upward. Review other orientations and recessed-pocket supports in your slicer. Fit your movement and hands after printing. Bambu A1 with one AMS Lite supports up to four filaments; gradients become solid colors. Other software can use standard 3MF textures if it supports them. Embed linked SVG images and convert text to paths for consistent lettering.
